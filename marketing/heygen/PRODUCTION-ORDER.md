## Order kis logic pe hai

Ye order teen cheezon pe banaya hai, isi priority me:

1. **Business logic** — pehle wo videos jo batayein *ye kya hai*, phir *safe hai*, phir
   *kaam ka hai*, phir emotion. Agar Wave 1 me trust video nahi hoga to log install
   karke bhi documents upload nahi karenge.
2. **Production difficulty** — shuru me aasaan videos. Jo videos sensitive hain (V27
   hospital, V57 parents, V83 health) unhe **baad me** rakha hai, jab tone ka andaza aa
   chuka ho. Pehli video me hi mushkil tone try karoge to kharab banegi.
3. **Blockers** — jin videos ke liye feature abhi nahi hai, wo **Wave 9 me** hain. Unse
   pehle ke 80 videos bina kisi rukavat ke ban sakte hain.
